This is the loneliest place on Earth. Sometimes, the nearest humans are in space.

Point [Nemo](/nˈimO/), in the South Pacific, is the spot farthest from any land. The nearest land is two thousand, six hundred and eighty-eight kilometers away: three tiny islands, none of them inhabited. In Latin, [nemo](/nˈimO/) means no one.

When the International Space Station passes overhead, the closest people to you are the astronauts in orbit.

Ships almost never come here, so space agencies use it as a graveyard for spacecraft. In two thousand one, the Russian space station [Mir](/mˈiɹ/) came down in this region. And in the early twenty thirties, the space station itself is planned to end its journey here.
