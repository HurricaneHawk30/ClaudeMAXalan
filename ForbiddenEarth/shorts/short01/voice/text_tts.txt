This town has been on fire since nineteen sixty-two. And it's still burning.

In May nineteen sixty-two, [Centralia](/sɛntɹˈAliə/), Pennsylvania, burned trash in an old mining pit. The leading theory is that the fire reached a coal seam underground. It crept through the abandoned mines beneath the town, sending smoke and toxic gases up through cracks in the ground. Sinkholes opened in people's yards.

In nineteen eighty, about a thousand people lived here. The government paid most of them to leave. The town even lost its ZIP code. By twenty twenty, only five residents were left.

And the fire? Some estimates say it could keep burning for another two hundred and fifty years.
