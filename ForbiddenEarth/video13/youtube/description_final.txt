Some roads take you somewhere. These ten roads might not let you come back. A highway built across frozen lakes that melts away every spring. A tunnel that villagers carved through a cliff with hammers and chisels. A road in Siberia that people call the Road of Bones. And at number one: a narrow strip of dirt in the Andes so deadly that a major bank named it the most dangerous road in the world.
